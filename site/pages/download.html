Get GeoTag Camera

Free, about 15 MB, for Android 8.0 or newer. Right now it installs from a signed APK on GitHub; F-Droid and Google Play are coming soon.

Coming from 1.3.0 or older? Uninstall it first. Those builds used a development key, so Android can't update them in place. Your photos stay in the gallery and still verify; after installing, Gallery, Import photos adds them back to the list. From 1.4.0 on, updates install over the top.

Where to get it

GeoTag Camera is published only on GitHub, F-Droid and Google Play. An APK from anywhere else is not from this project. The F-Droid build will carry the same signature as GitHub's (the builds are reproducible), so you can switch between them without reinstalling.

Which APK do I need?

Pick your phone. Not sure? Most phones from the last several years are the first one.

geotagcamera-v{{version}}-arm64-v8a.apk

64-bit ARM: nearly every phone and tablet sold in recent years. About 15 MB.

Download APK SHA-256

Want to be sure? With USB debugging on, adb shell getprop ro.product.cpu.abi prints your phone's type. All files are on the latest release page.

Install

  1. Download the APK on your phone, or copy it over from your computer.
  2. Open it from your browser's downloads or a file manager.
  3. The first time, Android asks to allow installs from that app. Allow it for that app only, go back, and tap Install.
  4. Open GeoTag Camera, choose Field or Professional, and allow camera and location.
  5. Wait for "Location locked" in the corner of the viewfinder, then take your first photo.

Play Protect may warn about an app it hasn't seen before. That's normal for apps installed outside the Play Store; you can check the file with its SHA-256 below.

Check the download (optional)

Each APK has a matching .sha256 file. Put both in the same folder and run:

Get-FileHash .\geotagcamera-v{{version}}-arm64-v8a.apk -Algorithm SHA256
Get-Content .\geotagcamera-v{{version}}-arm64-v8a.apk.sha256

In PowerShell. The two values must match (case doesn't matter).

shasum -a 256 -c geotagcamera-v{{version}}-arm64-v8a.apk.sha256

Prints OK when the file is intact.

sha256sum -c geotagcamera-v{{version}}-arm64-v8a.apk.sha256

Prints OK when the file is intact.

Updating

Every release from 1.4.0 on is signed with the same key, so a newer APK installs over the old one and keeps your photos, projects and settings. To hear about new versions, choose Watch, Custom, Releases on the GitHub repository. Once F-Droid is live, its app will update GeoTag Camera for you, and it installs over a GitHub APK. Google Play will be a separate listing with its own updates.

System requirements

  • Android 8.0 or newerAPI 26 and up, phones and tablets.
  • Camera and GPSApproximate-only location works, with less precise stamps.
  • No Google Play servicesThe GitHub and F-Droid versions run on de-Googled phones.
  • Internet optionalFor the stamp map, offline map downloads, weather and sending reports.
  • StorageAbout 15 MB, plus up to about 150 MB of map cache and saved areas.
  • PermissionsCamera, location while the camera is open, photo location (for Verify).
