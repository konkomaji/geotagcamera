Tamper-evident

Edit the photo. Watch the proof break.

Every photo is hashed and signed on the phone, together with its GPS location and time. Change one pixel, crop it, or rewrite its location, and the signature no longer matches. Anyone can check, offline, on any phone.

  1. Shoot

    The stamp is burned in, then the image is hashed with SHA-256.

  2. Sign

    A key created in your phone's secure keystore signs the hash plus latitude, longitude and time. The key never leaves the phone.

  3. Verify

    The proof travels inside the file. Open it in the app's Verify screen to see whether it is unchanged and which key signed it.

Real problems, fixed

The complaints in every GPS camera app's reviews

Pulled from Play Store reviews of the most-installed GPS camera apps. Tap one to see how GeoTag Camera handles it.

An ad plays before the camera even opens.

No ads, ever. The app opens straight to the viewfinder. Nothing sits over the shutter, and nothing pops up between shots.

Basic stamp fields need a subscription.

Every field is free. Coordinates, address, map, altitude, bearing, logo, custom text, exports and PDF reports. Nothing is locked, and there's no paid version.

The address is from where I was five minutes ago.

Looked up for where you are. The address is fetched again as soon as you move about 30 m, and a GPS fix older than 30 seconds is never stamped.

The shot was lost while the GPS was still searching.

The photo is held, not lost. Take the shot anyway: it waits up to a minute for a GPS fix and saves the moment one arrives. You decide whether to keep waiting.

Zoom and focus are clumsy.

Pinch, zoom chips and tap to focus. Night and HDR modes too, on phones whose camera supports them.

No signal on site, so no address and no map.

Works offline. Stamping, signing and verifying need no connection. Addresses already looked up are reused, the Plus Code is worked out on the phone, and you can save map areas before a trip.

It drains the battery.

Location only while the camera is open. No background location and no background service. GPS stops the moment you leave the camera screen.

It took a photo the second it opened, and stopped my music.

Only you press the shutter. The app never shoots on its own, and it doesn't take over the phone's audio, so music and podcasts keep playing.

Renaming hundreds of photos for the report.

Named for you. Files are saved as IMG_20261109_104200_Kalna.jpg, grouped by project, and exported to CSV, KML, GeoJSON or a PDF site report.

Anyone can fake a GPS stamp.

Signed on the phone. Each photo is signed with its location and time; any later edit shows up when it's verified. Locations from mock-GPS apps are never signed.

Too many permissions and trackers for a camera.

Camera and location, that's it. Plus photo location for Verify. The GitHub and F-Droid versions contain no analytics or ad code; the source is public.

It crashes halfway through a job.

Built not to lose your work. If the phone runs out of memory the photo is saved at half size instead of dropped, and storage errors show a message instead of closing the app.
