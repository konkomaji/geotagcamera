Tamper-evident

Edit the photo. Watch the proof break.

Every photo is hashed and signed on the phone, together with its GPS location and time. Change one pixel, crop it, or rewrite its location, and the signature no longer matches. Anyone can check, offline, on any phone.

  1. Shoot

    The stamp is burned in, then the image is hashed with SHA-256.

  2. Sign

    A key created in your phone's secure keystore signs the hash plus latitude, longitude and time. The key never leaves the phone.

  3. Verify

    The proof travels inside the file. Open it in the app's Verify screen to see whether it is unchanged and which key signed it.

Built for field work

For people who need proof, not clutter

Inspectors and construction

Site photos with the address, a note like "Pole 12, north wall" and a signature, grouped by project.

Surveyors

Exact coordinates, sea-level altitude, accuracy and compass bearing, exported to CSV, KML or GeoJSON.

NGO and government teams

Works offline where there's no signal, with your organisation's logo and name on every photo, for free.

Students

Fieldwork photos with a PDF report that's ready to submit, without watching an ad first.

Live stamp preview

The stamp is drawn on the viewfinder by the same code that burns it in. What you frame is what you get.

Exports and PDF reports

CSV for Excel, KML for Google Earth, GeoJSON for QGIS, and a PDF site report with an overview map.

Respects your phone

Location only while the camera is open, never in the background. No account, no background service, no Google Play services needed.
