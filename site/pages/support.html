Support

Something not working?

There's no help desk, it's one person, but every report gets read. The clearer the report, the faster the fix.

Report a problem

From the app Best

Settings, then Report a problem. Describe what happened, add screenshots or a screen recording, press Send. It reaches me directly, no email app needed. You see everything included before it goes; screenshots have their hidden data, including GPS, removed first. After a crash or freeze, the app offers to send the details.

By email

Write to hello@konkomaji.com. The button starts a message with a short template. Please don't attach photos that show where people live unless they're needed.

Email a problem report

On GitHub

A public issue lets others with the same problem find it and follow the fix. Search the existing issues first. Ideas and questions are welcome there too.

Open an issue

What to include

  • The app version (About & legal shows it), your phone model and Android version. The in-app report adds these for you.
  • What you did, step by step, what happened, and what you expected.
  • For a verification problem: how the photo reached you. Many apps recompress photos, which breaks verification on purpose.
  • A screenshot or screen recording if it shows the problem. Crop out anything private.

Check the FAQ first, and if you upgraded from 1.3.0 or older, the uninstall-first note.

Security problems

Please don't open a public issue. Report it privately through a GitHub security advisory, or by email.

In scope: a way to make an edited photo verify, location or personal data leaving the phone in a way the privacy policy doesn't describe, crafted files or share intents that crash the app or read files they shouldn't, and anything that exposes the signing key. The full policy is in SECURITY.md. There's no bug bounty; reports are acknowledged within a week and credited unless you'd rather not be.
